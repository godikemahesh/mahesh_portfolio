Camp III · First real climb

Two months in,
they gave me the keys.

May 2025. I joined Valkontek Embedded & IoT as an intern working on computer vision for cars. No slides, no demos. Real cameras, real vehicles, real latency budgets.

I shipped end to end, from data to deployment on NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin, and two months later I was an AI Engineer. After that came person re-identification across eight live feeds, and agents my whole team used every day.
